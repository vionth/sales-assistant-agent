# Refund Policy

Refund requests must be raised within 30 days of the invoice date.
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7 working days
of approval.

Subscription customers who cancel mid-cycle receive a pro-rata refund for
the unused period. Setup and onboarding fees are non-refundable once
implementation has started.

Approval: refunds above INR 50,000 require sales manager sign-off.
