# Collections and Escalation

Day 1 overdue: automated reminder email to the billing contact.
Day 7 overdue: the account owner calls the customer and logs the outcome.
Day 15 overdue: sales manager is copied on all further correspondence.
Day 30 overdue: account is placed on hold and no new orders are accepted.

Any message or outreach that commits to a discount or a revised payment plan
requires written manager approval before it is sent to the customer.
