# Payment Terms

Standard payment terms are Net 30 from the invoice date.
Enterprise accounts may negotiate Net 45 with prior approval.

An invoice becomes overdue the day after its due date. A late payment
charge of 1.5% per month applies to overdue balances.

Accepted methods: NEFT, RTGS, UPI and corporate credit card.
Partial payments are accepted but do not stop late payment charges on the
remaining balance.
